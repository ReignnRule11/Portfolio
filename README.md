## 🏗️ Engineering Philosophy

I approach software development from the **system level**, not just the feature level.

### My Core Principles

1. **Architecture before implementation** — Understand the problem, constraints, actors, data flows, failure modes, and scalability requirements before writing large amounts of code.

2. **Build for real-world constraints** — Connectivity, device limitations, operational complexity, security, cost, and user behavior matter just as much as technical elegance.

3. **Offline-first when the environment demands it** — Connectivity should not automatically become a single point of failure. Design resilience into the architecture.

4. **AI should solve problems, not decorate products** — AI needs to produce measurable user or business value. Not every product needs AI.

5. **Security is part of architecture** — Authentication, authorization, privacy, data protection, auditability, and threat modeling belong in the design from the beginning.

6. **Product thinking matters** — A technically impressive product that nobody needs is still a failed product. Always validate the problem first.
