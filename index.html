Based in Lagos. Building Quorum—a transparent voting platform for events and elections. 7+ years shipping across mobile, backend, frontend, and product. I make hard trade-offs under constraint and build for real problems with proof of user adoption.

Work

Quorum

Problem

Voting tools were fragmented and broken. People didn't trust the systems. We needed something transparent and verifiable—a single platform for any event, any election.

What I Built

Started by mapping every screen and nailing the interface. Then hit the wall: blockchain integration was a bottleneck. Made the hard call: ship mobile-first on Expo instead, get iOS and Android out fast, test the market, see what users actually want, iterate faster. Built with React Native, Expo, and a focus on shipping over perfection.

Outcome

It worked. Users adopted it, event volume went up. Now we're adding blockchain back in because we've got proof it works and know exactly what people need. Built a company by shipping, measuring, and iterating—not by planning everything upfront.
